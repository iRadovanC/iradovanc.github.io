# Grafické podklady — IRON FRONT

Všechny níže uvedené finální obrázky byly vytvořeny vestavěným nástrojem **imagegen**. Nebylo použito externí API ani CLI. Obrázky jsou uloženy přímo v projektu a při hraní se nic nestahuje.

| Soubor | Použití |
| --- | --- |
| [terrain.png](terrain.png) | Generovaná textura půdy; Canvas ji kombinuje s cestami, řekou, mosty a vegetací. |
| [buildings.png](buildings.png) | Osm budov s průhledností; atlas 4 × 2. |
| [vehicles-cz.png](vehicles-cz.png) | Používaný atlas: Leopard 2A8, Iveco LMV a Tatra 815-7 pro AČR; bezejmenná protistrana má starší siluety a červené pruhy. |
| [quarry.png](quarry.png) | Průhledný terénní podklad lomu s rovnou pracovní plochou, nízkým skalním okrajem a otevřeným vjezdem ze západu. |
| [vehicles.png](vehicles.png) | Archiv původního atlasu vozidel; hra nyní načítá vehicles-cz.png. |

Výstupy jsou zachovány v původním PNG s alfa kanálem, pokud jej mají. Výřezy atlasů se provádějí při vykreslení pomocí Canvas drawImage. Textury jsou původní; nejsou převzaty z Red Alert ani jiné hry.

Budovy používají pouze kontaktní stínování obsažené v původním atlasu. Přidané odsazené ovály pod budovami byly odstraněny. Podklad lomu se vykresluje do terénu před jednotkami; Tatra po jeho rovné ploše projíždí v běžné úrovni terénu.

[Přesné prompty pro vehicles-cz.png a quarry.png](czech-prompts.md).

## Finální prompt: terrain.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless ground texture for a polished browser real-time strategy game. Primary request: a square seamless tileable texture of dry olive-brown steppe ground, military battlefield terrain, viewed from perfectly vertical top-down orthographic camera. Weathered muted khaki dirt, fine sand, scattered tiny gravel, subtle dried grass patches and small dark scrub tufts. Detailed hand-painted realistic strategy game terrain, atmospheric and natural, subdued low contrast so game units will stand out. Uniform scale across the entire image, no large distinctive features, no perspective, no horizon. Fine soil detail, beautiful organic material variation, sunlight from upper left. Restrained earthy tan, desaturated sage, ochre, brown palette. Fill the entire square edge to edge. No buildings, no vehicles, no roads, no craters, no rocks larger than gravel, no labels, no borders, no grid, no watermark. Generate at 1024x1024 or greater square resolution.

## Finální prompt: buildings.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual transparent sprite atlas for a 2D military real-time strategy game. Primary request: exactly EIGHT individual highly detailed military building sprites arranged in an EXACT 4-column by 2-row regular grid. Wide image ratio 2:1. Every building centered within its own equal-sized cell and wholly contained in that cell, at least 12 percent empty margin all sides. No text, no labels, no borders, no connecting ground between cells. Transparent background with true alpha, not checkerboard. All buildings share fixed orthographic isometric 3/4 top-down camera, visible front and right sides, roof visible, military olive green/weathered concrete with small muted turquoise team accent stripes. Serious premium classic late-1990s RTS remastered aesthetic, finely rendered 3D pixel-sharp game assets with physically detailed metal panels, vents, cables, pipes, rooftop details and subtle ambient shadows. Small square foundation pads part of each building only. First row left to right: 1 command headquarters bunker with large satellite dish and antenna, 2 electric power plant with two cooling stacks and glowing teal coil, 3 ore refinery with cylindrical tanks, conveyor and yellow hazard stripes, 4 infantry barracks with olive tent roof and equipment crates. Second row left to right: 5 large war factory with armored hangar and open garage door, 6 small defensive turret emplacement with long twin autocannon, 7 radar communications tower with large dish and four support feet, 8 imposing enemy fortress command bunker in concrete and RED accent stripes, multiple red antenna lights. Each building single isolated asset. Precise regular atlas spacing with four cells per row. Ground texture outside the foundation must be fully transparent. No vehicles, no humans, no UI. Beautiful professional production game sprites.

## Finální prompt: vehicles.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent vehicle sprite atlas for a military 2D real-time strategy game. Primary request: six individual professional detailed military vehicle sprites in an EXACT 3-column by 2-row uniform grid, each isolated and centered in its equal cell with generous 15 percent transparent empty margin. Wide image, aspect ratio 3:2. All vehicles seen perfectly vertically from above in TRUE TOP DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC view, no isometric perspective. Every vehicle faces RIGHT horizontally, nose and barrels point to the right edge of the image. Remastered classic RTS aesthetic, realistic weathered painted armor, tread links, grilles, hatches and gun barrel details. Dark olive green and warm khaki steel, small turquoise accent team stripes on first row and small muted red accent team stripes on second row. First row left to right: 1 heavily armored main battle tank with one long turret cannon facing right and wide detailed tracks, 2 compact four-wheel armored reconnaissance jeep with machine gun and rugged tires, 3 industrial tracked ore harvester with rear cargo bed full of golden minerals and yellow hazard stripes on front cab. Second row: the same three vehicle designs in the same order but enemy versions with RED team stripes in place of turquoise stripes. Each sprite centered exactly in its grid cell, entirely inside cell with clean transparent space on all sides. Vehicles fill about 75 percent of cell width. All facing RIGHT. True alpha transparent background, no checkerboard, no ground plane, no text, no shadows detached from vehicles, no labels, no border. Crisp legible small game asset, highly detailed and physically convincing.
